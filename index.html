How it works

Here's what working
with us looks like.

Three steps, shaped around how your business actually works.

01
Current State Assessment

We come in (or hop on a call), shadow your team, and document every process that involves manual work. We map where time is leaking — data entry, missed follow-ups, disconnected tools, slow handoffs — and show you exactly what it's costing in real hours.

02
Solution Design & Build

We design the automation and build it. Using your existing tools wherever possible, we layer in AI where it makes a real difference. We work alongside you the whole way to make sure you get exactly what you're envisioning. First automations are usually live within two to four weeks.

03
Handoff & Ongoing Support

Every build is documented and handed off running. We don't disappear. We stay on as your automation partner — monitoring, expanding, and adapting as your business grows. No new software for your team to learn.
